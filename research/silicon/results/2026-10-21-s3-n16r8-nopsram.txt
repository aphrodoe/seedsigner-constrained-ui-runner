# Why int_low halved when the renderer joined: not because the session needs 98 KB more. At "psbt parse"
# one allocation missed in the existing heap areas, and MicroPython's split heap (SPLIT_HEAP_AUTO, always on
# in the esp32 port) then grows by MIN(largest free system block, MAX(current heap, request)) -- roughly
# doubling, py/gc.c gc_try_add_heap. It took a 131,076 B area, and gc_free right after shows 170 KB of the
# Python heap still empty. The live peak is gc_alloc 135,584. Whether a miss happens depends on heap layout:
# the earlier run below, without the renderer's 14 KB resident, never missed. profile_embit (GC off) shows the
# same 131,076 B step. So int_low is set by the growth policy; the real requirement is ~136 KB of Python heap.
